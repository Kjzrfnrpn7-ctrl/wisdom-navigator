# Case Symbol Map Standard

Every independent professional case counted toward the research corpus should
produce one case-level symbol map in the same research batch.

## Required layers

1. **Observed** — only facts supported by the case source.
2. **Abstracted mechanism** — the project’s interpretation of how the case works.
3. **Multi-parameter decomposition** — actors, agency, goal, relation, trigger,
   obstacle/constraint, evidence and payoff where applicable.
4. **Relational rule** — the transferable relationship, not the visual shell.
5. **Mother mechanism** — the most compact mechanism family supported so far.
6. **Prior-art position** — predecessor / same-family / no-close-predecessor-found;
   absence of a predecessor is not proof of originality.
7. **Transfer space** — at least surface, task, relationship and system-level
   analogies when the mechanism supports them.
8. **Execution path** — a direct path from mechanism to an executable sequence.
9. **Do-not-copy boundary** — what belongs to the source case and must not be
   copied as style, wording, character, layout or staging.

## Counting rule

```text
SEARCH → DECONSTRUCT → SYMBOLIZE
```

A case is only counted as an effective R1 case after all three steps are
complete. A case without a symbol map remains `INCOMPLETE_CASE`.

## Evidence boundary

Observed, Abstracted and Derived content must remain distinguishable.
A symbol map is a research artifact, not proof that a mechanism is novel or
that it will transfer successfully into another domain.
