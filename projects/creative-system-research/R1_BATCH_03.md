## Prior-art corrections

The deep check again reduced false novelty:

- disguised hearing tests existed years before newer music-led versions;
- blank newspaper protest has a long historical lineage;
- real-time sound-to-haptic translation predates sports deployments;
- data sonification predates newer cultural sampling systems;
- packaging second-life and edible-packaging concepts predate newer award cases;
- spectacular B2B product-proof campaigns have mature predecessors;
- alternative low-plastic multipack systems predate later Fit Pack executions.

## Language-system implication

The language track now has a stronger candidate mechanism:

**Fixed Frame × Human Motive**

A stable institutional or administrative sentence can act as a scalable copy
system when the rigid answer categories are replaced by real human motives,
scenes and persona-specific payoffs.

This is a candidate mechanism shared by existing language projects. It is **not**
a justification for creating a new top-level language runtime.
