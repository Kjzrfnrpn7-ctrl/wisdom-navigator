## Prior-art findings

All 15 Batch-01 cases received a first deep prior-art pass.

Important corrections included:

- some apparently novel reveal mechanisms had close structural predecessors;
- some systemic ideas were better understood as new domain applications of
  older mechanism families;
- media self-demonstration had a much older internal lineage than a recent
  award-winning example suggested;
- voice-based diagnostic screening had clear scientific precedents, so the
  novelty claim belongs at the disease/application layer rather than the
  underlying signal-analysis mechanism.

These are **research candidates**, not canonical rules.
