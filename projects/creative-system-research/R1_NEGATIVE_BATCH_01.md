# R1 Negative Batch 01 — Counterexample Set

**Batch size:** 10 cases  
**R1 total after batch:** 43 / 200 independent professional cases

This set is intentionally not a collection of "brand fails." Its purpose is to
stress-test the mechanism library by recording cases where a strong hook,
metaphor, participation loop, identity change or AI capability produced a
misread, backlash, withdrawal, apology or corrective clarification.

## Failure families introduced

- social-issue appropriation without system change;
- value inversion by metaphor;
- AI replacing the meaningful human act;
- hook-context collapse;
- open participation without an adversarial model;
- identity change without continuity;
- AI diversity-claim overreach;
- scene / relationship misread;
- transformation metaphor colliding with cultural history;
- copy × visual interaction ambiguity.

## Why these matter

The negative set directly falsifies several tempting shortcuts:

- a powerful visual metaphor is not automatically good value proof;
- participation is not automatically a positive loop;
- provocative hooks must survive isolation from their context;
- AI usefulness depends on whether the automated step is friction or meaning;
- a brand-purpose message without internal or product truth is structurally weak;
- copy cannot be validated separately from casting, sequence and visual context.

## Research rule

Negative evidence participates in mechanism decisions at the same level as
award-winning cases. It is not relegated to an appendix.

The next step is to create **contrast sets** that pair a positive mechanism with
a structurally related failure, so that gates are learned from differences rather
than from success stories alone.
